## 摘要
在机器学习中，识别哪些变量提供预测信息，可能与预测准确性同样重要。在许多中小型表格数据集上，表格基础模型的预测性能已达到或超过调优后的梯度提升树。然而，它们在恢复总体水平变量重要性方面的能力却鲜受关注。我们设计并开展了一项蒙特卡洛模拟研究，比较表格基础模型与已有机器学习基线在变量重要性恢复方面的表现。我们的框架区分了多个总体估计量，为每个估计量匹配一个对齐的模型无关重要性估计器，并从排序、支持集和相对大小三个方面评估恢复效果。结果表明，在恢复总体变量重要性方面，表格基础模型通常与已有机器学习基线具有竞争力，且往往更准确。因此，我们将其能力证据扩展到一个此前研究不足的维度，进一步增强了其吸引力。

## Abstract
In machine learning, identifying which variables contribute predictive information can be as important as predictive accuracy. Tabular foundation models have matched or exceeded the predictive performance of tuned gradient boosting trees on many small- and medium-sized tabular datasets. Yet their ability to recover population-level variable importance has received little attention. We design and conduct a Monte Carlo simulation study to compare variable importance recovery across tabular foundation models and established machine learning baselines. Our framework distinguishes multiple population estimands, pairs each one with an aligned model-agnostic importance estimator, and evaluates recovery in terms of ranking, support, and relative magnitude. Our results show that tabular foundation models are generally competitive with established machine-learning baselines in recovering population variable importance, and often more accurate. We thus extend the evidence for their capabilities to a previously underexamined dimension, further strengthening their appeal.
