## 摘要
李雅普诺夫函数是动力系统稳定性分析的基石，但合成其符号形式仍然困难，因为不存在通用的构造算法。为应对这一挑战，我们提出SLyM，一个利用大语言模型（LLM）的知识与推理能力来发现符号李雅普诺夫函数的框架。为避免让LLM承担其并不擅长的数值生成任务，SLyM将构造过程分解为结构提议与系数优化：LLM依据其关于李雅普诺夫构造的先验知识提出函数结构，随后由凸规划求得其系数。局部搜索得到的结构被回馈到监督中，从而将覆盖范围扩展到初始提议器未能覆盖的系统。在所有基准上，SLyM均持续优于所评估的神经网络、平方和以及生成式基线方法。

## Abstract
Lyapunov functions are the cornerstone of stability analysis for dynamical systems, yet synthesizing a symbolic form remains difficult as no universal constructive algorithm exists. To address this challenge, we introduce SLyM, a framework that harnesses the knowledge and reasoning of large language models (LLMs) to discover symbolic Lyapunov functions. To avoid charging the LLM with ill-suited numerical generation, SLyM decomposes the construction into structure proposal and coefficient optimization: the LLM draws on its prior knowledge of Lyapunov constructions to propose the function structure, whose coefficients then follow from a convex program. Local-search structures are folded back into supervision, extending coverage to systems missed by the initial proposer. Across all benchmarks, SLyM consistently outperforms the evaluated neural, sum-of-squares, and generative baselines.
